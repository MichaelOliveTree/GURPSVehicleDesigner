   News 
 

 

November 10, 1999 -  GVD Version 1.1.6 Released !

  • This is the first major upgrade since 1.0 was released back in September.   New features include Copy/Paste support for individual vehicle components, Saving/Loading of individual components, more performance profile options, and more.   Visit the Revision History page for a complete list of all t he new features and fixes.

 

 

October 8, 1999 - GVD Homepage joins the GURPS Webring

  • GVD has joined the GURPS Webring.  If you have a GURPS page and want to join the ring click here! 

 

 

October 5, 1999 -  GURPS Vehicle Designer 1.0.3 Released!

  • The Online and Voice ordering through RegSoft are now active.  Click here to go to the Ordering and Registration information page.

 

 

September 28, 1999 -  Join the GVD mailing list!

  • The GVD mailing has been formed egroups for general gvd discussions, suggestions, status updates, bug reports, etc.  Joining the group is easy.  Just send mail to gvd-subscribe@egroups.com   All email that is sent to you from the egroup can easily be sorted by your email client since all gvd messages have "gvd@egroups.com" in the TO field. Configuring your email client to sort all incoming messages from that address should be a snap.

 

 

September 25, 1999 -  GURPS Vehicle Designer 1.0.24 Released!

  • GVD is now better than ever.  Special thanks to everyone who took time out to send in bug reports.  This newest version fixes quite a few problems and adds a couple of new features as well.  Please check out the Program History page for a complete list of whats changed in this latest build.

 

 

September 21, 1999 -  Secure Online Ordering available via RegSoft

  • Secure online ordering is now available via Regsoft.  Click here to order your registered copy of GVD.

 

 

September 19, 1999 -  GURPS Vehicle Designer 1.0.18 Released!

  • Thanks for the bug reports.  Fortunately these were all minor problems and were quick and easy to fix.  At this point all known bugs have been fixed.   Check the Revision History for more information on what's changed in version 1.0.18. Click [here!] to download the new version.

 

 

September 17, 1999 -  GURPS Vehicle Designer 1.0 Released!

  • GURPS Vehicle Designer has been approved by Steve Jackson Games and the first public release version has been made available.   Get it [here!]

 

August 22, 1999 -   GURPS Vehicle Designer 1.0 submitted to Steve Jackson Games for approval

  • GVD version 1.0 has been submitted to Steve Jackson Games for evaluation.  If all goes well, the program will be approved and can soon be to released it to the public.
